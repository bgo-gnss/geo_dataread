# Destination: geo_dataread data→plot baseline (verify + characterize, pre-§10.1)

## Problem
The GPS **data → plot** happy path already exists and is wired
(`getData(sta, tType="TOT")` → `openGlobkTimes` → plate-subtract → `toDateTime`
→ the just-modernized `gps_plot`), reading the **original GLOBK solutions** at
`/mnt_data/gpsdata` (`mb_STA_TOT.dat{1,2,3}`, 3 header lines — `skiprows=3` is
correct). Before the §10.1 pure-I/O refactor (B), establish a **trustworthy,
real-data-verified baseline**: prove the chain on real stations, pin it with
real fixtures, and characterize the one load-bearing discrepancy — without
touching any production output.

## In scope (A)
- **Real-data end-to-end verification** — `station → getData → modernized plot`
  on a handful of real `/mnt_data/gpsdata` stations, eyeballed (same by-eye loop
  as gps_plot).
- **Real-data golden fixtures** — extend the Phase-0 masters (`21bf3ef`) with
  2–3 real stations (frozen); pins the read path against production data.
- **Characterize the drift (doc-only)** — precise diff of `gamittoNEU` (.NEU:
  subtract plate in m, then ×1000) vs `getData` (plot: ×1000, then subtract
  plate×1000): numerically equivalent or not, and exactly where they diverge.
- **Per-item fix/defer ledger** — each finding tagged *fix-in-A* (safe, off-.NEU,
  moves no master) or *defer-to-B* (moves a master / touches .NEU).

## Out of scope
- The §10.1 pure-I/O refactor, dead-code deletion, moving math to `gps_analysis`.
- **ANY change to `gamittoNEU`/`gamittooneuf`/`.NEU` output** → B + explicit sign-off
  (it feeds `.NEU → cdn.vedur.is`; brunnur/aflogun consume it).
- Research-only `read_gps_data`/detrend machinery.
- `tType="JOIN"`/`08h` branch — **BGÓ uses it and will again**: do NOT delete;
  leave crash-pinned; revive in a later slice (not first priority).

## Definition of done
- data→plot verified **by-eye** on several real stations.
- 2–3 real-data golden fixtures added and passing.
- Drift characterized in writing (equivalent-or-not + divergence points).
- Fix/defer ledger produced; every A-fix **moves no golden master and touches no
  `.NEU`** (the tripwire).

## Hard constraints
- `/mnt_data/gpsdata` = original GLOBK solutions, **read-only** production input.
- Golden masters (`21bf3ef`) are the **tripwire**: a fix that moves one is out of A.
- `gamittooneuf → gamittoNEU` is the `.NEU` production path — its output must not
  change in A.
- Human by-eye sign-off.
